Fried Egg Filipino

Description

Fried Egg Filipino features eggs fried until edges are crispy and golden while centers remain soft.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Crack eggs into hot oil.
  3. Cook until edges are crispy and golden.
  4. Season with salt.
  5. Serve.
