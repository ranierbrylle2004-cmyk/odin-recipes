Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken Adobo features chicken braised in soy sauce and vinegar.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken pieces
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Combine chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and pepper in a pot.
  2. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 30 minutes.
  3. Serve.
